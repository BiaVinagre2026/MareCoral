## Prompt da Coral Real v1

```text
Use case: identity-preserve
Asset type: master identity reference sheet for a digital fashion ambassador
Input images: Image 1 is the Coral Ícone reference; use its recognizable facial traits, warm personality, long highlighted brown hair, medium tan skin and athletic identity as inspiration, while converting the character into a believable photorealistic adult human.
Primary request: create the first identity sheet for “Coral Real”, the consistent digital ambassador of Maré Coral Fitwear.
Scene/backdrop: seamless warm off-white studio background, neutral and uncluttered.
Subject: one Brazilian woman, apparent age 30–34, athletic natural healthy proportions, medium tan skin, brown eyes, long dark brown hair with subtle caramel highlights, approachable confident expression. She wears a simple unbranded coral-and-rose athletic set with a high-waist legging and supportive sports top.
Style/medium: polished photorealistic fashion casting photography with real skin texture, natural hair strands and realistic performance fabric; premium but not over-retouched.
Composition/framing: a clean four-panel character reference sheet showing the exact same woman in every panel: frontal head-and-shoulders portrait; three-quarter head-and-shoulders portrait; full-body neutral front pose; full-body relaxed three-quarter pose. Keep complete head and feet visible in full-body panels.
Lighting/mood: soft diffused daylight studio lighting, warm, young, sporty and carioca.
Color palette: coral and rose clothing, warm sand backdrop, small neutral graphite details only.
Constraints: identity must be consistent across all four panels; realistic human anatomy and hands; natural athletic body; no caricature, no doll proportions, no exaggerated muscles, no body reshaping between views, no logos, no jewelry, no bottle, no props, no text, no labels, no watermark. Do not reproduce the pedestal or gym objects from the reference image. This is an original digital persona, not a claim that a real person modeled the garment.
```

Geração feita com a ferramenta integrada de imagens e usando `coral-icone-v1.jpeg` como referência visual.
